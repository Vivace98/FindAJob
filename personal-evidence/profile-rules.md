## Aggregation rules

- Group by ontology `family`, then capability; do not create a capability entry for a method or tool alone.
- Use evidence count and diversity descriptively, not as a star score or universal proficiency number.
- Preserve original project context. Evidence of subgroup analysis in survey research does not become direct commercial segmentation evidence.
- A profile records demonstrated scope. It does not record absent, learnable, structural, or job-specific gaps; those belong to future Job Fit analysis.
